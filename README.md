## About

I'm an AI/ML engineer and researcher working at the intersection of deep learning, fuzzy systems, and real-world deployment. My current research focuses on Type-2 Fuzzy Neural Networks for smart HVAC control and NLP-based language model applications.

At GSU, I serve as a Graduate TA for the Deep Learning course — building rubrics, grading assignments on diffusion models and graph neural networks, and translating complex theory into structured, accessible feedback for students.

I build things that work: from research systems with real inference logic to full-stack applications. I'm targeting PhD programs for Fall 2027, with a research focus on agentic AI and edge-deployed ML systems.
